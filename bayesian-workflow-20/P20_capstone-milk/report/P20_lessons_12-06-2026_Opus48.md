# Lessons Report — Project 20 (Capstone): Primate Milk Energy

**Author:** Truong Van Thien, PhD · **Date:** 12-06-2026 · **Model tag:** Opus48

## 1. The concept taught
The capstone fuses the whole workflow: a **multivariate** regression with **Bayesian missing-data
imputation** (12 of 29 neocortex values missing), **PSIS-LOO model selection**, **power-scaling
sensitivity**, and **Simulation-Based Calibration** — and it showcases a **masking/suppression** effect
where two predictors that each look weak alone become clear together.

## 2. Workflow lessons from this run (with the actual numbers)
1. **Recovery first, then imputation:** the complete-case model recovered bN = 0.71, bM = −0.68, a, σ
   inside their 90% HDIs before imputation was added to real data.
2. **Impute inside the model:** all 29 species informed the fit despite 12 missing neocortex values —
   PyMC turned the missing entries into latent parameters (no dropping, no mean-fill). 0 divergences, R̂ = 1.00.
3. **The masking effect is the headline:** bN = **+0.48** (94% HDI +0.01, +0.89) and bM = **−0.54**
   (−0.93, −0.16). Each predictor's marginal scatter looks flat; only the joint model reveals that larger
   relative neocortex means *more* energetic milk while larger body mass means *less*.
4. **LOO confirms "both":** the two-predictor model wins (weight 1.0), beating mass-only by 2.47 ± 1.12 and
   neocortex-only by 3.82 ± 1.98 elpd. One Pareto-k̂ = 0.79 > 0.70 (n = 29 is small) is flagged honestly.
5. **Calibrated, and honest about its limits:** SBC ranks were uniform (chi-square p = 0.98); power-scaling
   kept the signs stable (bN 0.52→0.44, bM −0.57→−0.50) with the mild movement small samples warrant.

## 3. Common mistakes
- **Dropping incomplete rows** or **mean-filling** the missing neocortex — both throw away information and
  bias the uncertainty. Model the missing values instead.
- **Reading marginal scatterplots as evidence of no effect** — masking hides real associations until you
  condition on the confounder.
- **Ignoring a Pareto-k̂ > 0.7** — report it; consider `reloo`/moment-matching for the precise elpd.

## 4. Try it yourself

2. Add `clade` as a varying intercept and compare via LOO.
3. Refit complete-case (drop the 12 incomplete rows) and compare coefficient uncertainty — what did imputation buy?
