# Lessons Report — Project 11: Eight Schools (centered vs non-centered)

**Author:** Truong Van Thien, PhD · **Date:** 12-06-2026 · **Model tag:** Opus48

## 1. The concept taught
The first **hierarchical (multilevel) model** and the most famous **computational failure** in applied
Bayes: the *funnel*. The model is innocent; the **parameterization** decides whether HMC can sample it.
You learn to (a) recognize a funnel from divergences + low `tau` ESS, (b) visualize it with `plot_pair`
colored by divergence, and (c) **fix** it with the non-centered form. Tier 3 adds **SBC** to certify the
fix is actually calibrated, not just divergence-free.

## 2. Workflow lessons from this run (with the actual numbers)
1. **Recovery first:** simulating with `mu = 6, tau = 5` recovered both (6.04, 4.94) inside their 90% HDIs (non-centered model), R̂ = 1.00.
2. **The centered model genuinely fails:** **129 divergences**, tau ESS-bulk **215**, R̂(tau) = **1.02**. This is not a contrived toy — it is the canonical failure.
3. **The funnel is visible:** divergent draws sit at median `log(tau) = 0.22` versus **1.55** for healthy draws — they live in the narrow neck where `tau → 0`.
4. **The fix is dramatic and free:** non-centering gives **0 divergences** (from 129), tau ESS **2008** (×9), R̂ = 1.00 — the *same* posterior, sampled cleanly.
5. **The result is humble:** `mu = 6.40` (94% HDI **−1.28 to 14.35**, crossing 0); `tau = 4.90` (HDI 0.01 to 11.75). School A's raw +28 shrinks to **9.06**.
6. **Partial ≈ complete pooling here:** LOO `elpd_diff = 0.30 ± 0.18` (1.7 SE) — with J = 8 there is little evidence to prefer the hierarchy over one shared effect.
7. **SBC certifies the sampler:** rank histogram for `mu` is flat, chi-square p = **0.612** → calibrated.

## 3. Common mistakes
- **Trusting a divergent run.** 129 divergences invalidate the posterior; never report numbers from the centered fit.
- **Cranking `target_accept` to 0.99 instead of reparameterizing.** It reduces divergences slowly and at large speed cost; non-centering removes the cause.
- **Confusing computation with model.** Centered vs non-centered are the *same* model — do not "compare" them with LOO. Compare partial vs complete pooling instead.
- **Over-reading the ranking.** With `tau` barely identified, "school A is best" is not supported.

## 4. Try it yourself
1. Run SBC on **`tau`** instead of `mu` — is it still uniform? (`tau` is hard to identify with J = 8.)
2. Push the centered model to `target_accept = 0.99`; count divergences and the time penalty.
3. Add a 9th school with a tiny SE; watch its `theta` shrink less than the high-SE schools.
