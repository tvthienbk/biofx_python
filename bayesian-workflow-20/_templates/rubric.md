# Student Grading Rubric (per project hand-in) — 100 pts

| Criterion | Pts | What full marks looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Model specification & priors justified | 15 | sensible likelihood/link; priors defended; DAG correct |
| Prior predictive (outcome scale) | 10 | shows priors imply plausible data; fixes if not |
| Computation & diagnostics | 20 | passes/explains R̂, ESS, divergences, BFMI; remedies applied |
| Posterior interpretation (original units) | 15 | correct, uncertainty-honest, domain-meaningful |
| Posterior predictive check | 15 | appropriate discrepancy stat; identifies misfit |
| Model comparison / expansion | 10 | meaningful contrast; LOO read correctly |
| Sensitivity / SBC (tier-appropriate) | 10 | robustness assessed; SBC ranks read correctly |
| Communication & reproducibility | 5 | clean notebook; runs end-to-end; clear write-up |

*Adapt weights per tier (Tier 1 may drop SBC/comparison and redistribute to interpretation).*
