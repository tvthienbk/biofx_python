# References — Project 04: Divorce ~ Marriage + Age (multiple linear regression)

## (a) Dataset primary source
WaffleDivorce was compiled by Richard McElreath from **US CDC / National Center for Health Statistics**
(divorce and marriage rates) and **US Census** (median age at marriage) data. There is no single primary
journal article; the confounding analysis is the worked example of:
- **McElreath, R. (2020).** *Statistical Rethinking: A Bayesian Course with Examples in R and Stan*
  (2nd ed.), Chapter 5. CRC Press. **[textbook — cite + link]**

Distributed with McElreath's `rethinking` package (GPL-3): https://github.com/rmcelreath/rethinking
