# Lessons Report — Project 14: Tadpoles (hierarchical Binomial, shrinkage)

**Author:** Truong Van Thien, PhD · **Date:** 12-06-2026 · **Model tag:** Opus48

## 1. The concept taught
The first **multilevel** model. 48 tanks of tadpoles each get their own survival probability, but those
probabilities are tied together by an **adaptive prior** `a_tank ~ Normal(a_bar, sigma_tank)`. This is
**partial pooling**: every tank's estimate borrows strength from the rest, and the amount of borrowing
(**shrinkage**) is largest for the tanks with the least data. Two reusable skills: the **non-centered**
parameterization that keeps the sampler healthy, and **SBC** to prove the algorithm is calibrated.

## 2. Workflow lessons from this run (with the actual numbers)
1. **Recovery first:** simulating with a_bar = 1.4, sigma_tank = 1.0 recovered both (1.50, 0.78) inside
   their 90% HDIs before touching real data.
2. **Shrinkage ∝ 1/density:** grand-mean survival is **0.794** (a_bar = 1.35); low-density (n=10) tanks
   shrink toward it by **0.037** on average, vs only **0.017** for high-density (n=35) tanks. Small tanks
   carry less information, so the model trusts their raw proportions less.
3. **Across-tank variation is real and large:** **sigma_tank = 1.62** on the logit scale — tanks genuinely differ.
4. **Non-centering pays off:** with `a = a_bar + z·sigma`, the funnel disappears — **0 divergences**,
   BFMI 0.68–0.77, R̂ ≤ 1.01.
5. **Partial pooling decisively beats complete pooling:** LOO `elpd_diff` = **178.7** with SE 32.6
   (**5.5 SE**). (Max Pareto-k̂ = 1.34 flags a few influential tanks, so LOO is approximate — but the gap
   is far too large to overturn.)
6. **SBC passed:** 100 sims, chi-square uniformity **p = 0.784** — ranks are uniform, the sampler is calibrated.

## 3. Common mistakes
- **Using the centered parameterization** (`a_tank ~ Normal(a_bar, sigma_tank)`) and ignoring the resulting
  divergences in the funnel when sigma_tank is small.
- **Reading the raw per-tank proportion as truth** for small tanks — it is noisy; the pooled estimate is better.
- **Trusting LOO blindly** when Pareto-k̂ > 0.7 — here it is 1.34, so the LOO value is approximate (reloo to refine).

## 4. Try it yourself
1. Fit the centered parameterization and compare divergences and ESS.
2. Add `pred` (0/1) as a predictor of `a_tank`. Does `sigma_tank` shrink once predation is explained?
3. Plot shrinkage magnitude vs `1/density` and confirm small tanks shrink most.
