# Lessons Report — Project 09: Roaches (Poisson + offset → NegativeBinomial)

**Author:** Truong Van Thien, PhD · **Date:** 12-06-2026 · **Model tag:** Opus48

## 1. The concept taught
Two skills at once: an **exposure offset** (`log(exposure2)` with coefficient fixed at 1), and the
**diagnose-then-cure of overdispersion**. A PPC on the **proportion of zeros** exposes the failure of a
plain Poisson, and the cure — a **NegativeBinomial** — is confirmed by LOO.

## 2. Workflow lessons from this run (with the actual numbers)
1. **Recovery first:** simulating an overdispersed NegBin with a = 2.0, b1 = 0.8, b2 = -0.5 recovered
   all three (2.02, 0.730, -0.618) inside their 90% HDIs; R̂ = 1.00.
2. **The data scream overdispersion:** variance **2576** ≫ mean **25.6**, and **35.9%** of apartments
   caught zero roaches.
3. **The PPC is the whole lesson:** the Poisson predicts a proportion of zeros in **[0.000, 0.004]** —
   it essentially **never** produces a zero-roach apartment — when **0.359** were observed
   (**bayes-p = 0.000**). The NegBin predicts **[0.271, 0.405]**, covering the truth (bayes-p = 0.30).
4. **Overdispersion also distorts the effect and its uncertainty:** the Poisson gives a treatment rate
   ratio of **0.60** with a *falsely narrow* HDI (b2 sd = 0.025); the NegBin gives **0.48** (52% fewer
   roaches) with an honestly wider HDI (-1.19..-0.31). The dispersion **α = 0.27** quantifies the gap.
5. **LOO is unambiguous:** NegBin beats Poisson by `elpd_diff` = **5343 ± 706** (**7.6 SE**), stacking
   weight 0.98. (Max Pareto-k̂ = 0.74, just over threshold — reported honestly, but irrelevant given the
   size of the gap.)
6. **Prior is irrelevant:** power-scaling leaves E[b_treatment] at -0.747/-0.738/-0.728 across α.

## 3. Common mistakes
- **Trusting a Poisson's tiny standard errors** — overdispersion makes them dishonestly small.
- **Forgetting the offset** (or adding `log(exposure)` as a free coefficient when theory says it should be ~1).
- **Reading only the coefficient table** and skipping the zeros/max PPC that reveals the misfit.

## 4. Try it yourself
1. Fit a **zero-inflated NegBin**; does the structural-zero component beat the plain NegBin on LOO?
2. Make `log(exposure2)` a free coefficient; how close to 1 is its posterior, and why does that matter?
3. Report the treatment as a **% reduction** with a 90% interval; explain why the Poisson's interval was falsely narrow.

## 5. Self-check
1. What is an offset, and why is its coefficient fixed at 1?
2. The Poisson predicts ~0% zeros but 36% were observed. What does bayes-p = 0.000 tell you?
3. Why did the treatment rate ratio move from 0.60 (Poisson) to 0.48 (NegBin), and which do you trust?
4. What does the dispersion α = 0.27 mean, and what is the Poisson limit?
5. `elpd_diff` = 5343 ± 706. Is NegBin decisively better? How do you handle the Pareto-k̂ = 0.74?
