# Acknowledgments

This book stands on the open science of the protein-design community. The tools it teaches — RFdiffusion and its atom-level successors, ProteinMPNN and LigandMPNN, AlphaFold and Boltz — exist as usable software because their authors chose to release code, weights, and honest benchmarks. The field's culture of depositing structures, publishing failure rates, and arguing in public about controls is the reason a textbook like this can be written at all. Any errors of fact or emphasis are the author's own.
