# About the Author

This text is written from the perspective of a structural biologist who designs, expresses, and characterizes enzymes — someone who has watched confident models aggregate in the centrifuge and watched unpromising-looking designs turn over substrate. That dual vantage, computational and experimental, shapes every chapter: the *From the Bench* margin notes, the insistence on dead-mutant controls, and the refusal to call a design a success before a kinetic assay says so all come from the same conviction that the two halves of this field must be taught together.
